53 calls per doctor per day. Most of them are for one thing.

Patients get what they need without picking up the phone.

Hush handles the five reasons people call: appointments, refills, symptom questions, billing, and referrals. Routine requests are finished on the spot. Anything that needs a person goes into one organized queue for your staff, sorted by urgency, and comes with a callback time instead of hold music.

  • Book, move, or cancel from live open slots, with an automatic waitlist
  • Refill requests sent to the doctor's queue already filled out
  • Red-flag symptom screen first, so emergencies are sent to 911/988 right away
  • Scheduled callbacks moved away from the Monday-morning, after-lunch, and closing-time rushes

How to read this

  • Handled without staff means the patient finished the task alone, e.g. booked a slot or saw a referral's status.
  • Routed means it needs a person (refill sign-off, nurse callback) but arrives fully filled out, so it takes about 1 minute instead of a 4-minute call.
  • Staff cost uses the loaded hourly rate in Setup. Savings count phone time only; they leave out fewer missed calls and fewer no-shows.
